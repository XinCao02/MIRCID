# Caption for reference — FigureS_HubmiRFeatureAnalysis

**Supplementary Fig. X | Embedding-control robustness and linear recoverability of inferred HubmiR features.** **a,** Held-out pathway-classification accuracy for random forest (RF) and ResNet using Gene, Gene plus 414-dimensional principal-component (PCA), autoencoder (AE) or random-projection (RP) controls, or Gene plus inferred HubmiR. Boxes show the median and interquartile range, whiskers the minimum and maximum, open circles all seven displayed runs, and diamonds with bars the mean ± 1 s.d.; RP denotes the split-wise mean of five fixed projections. **b,** AE training and validation reconstruction mean-squared error across 66 epochs. The diamond marks the minimum validation error at epoch 54. **c,** Median held-out R² for each inferred miRNA, paired between prediction from the full Gene space and the 977-gene HubmiRNet input (n = 414 miRNAs); the dashed diagonal denotes equal recovery. **d,** Variance-weighted, uniform-average and median per-miRNA held-out R² from cross-validated multi-output Ridge models predicting all 414 HubmiR features from the two Gene source spaces. Bars show mean ± 1 s.d. across 20 frozen splits. Ridge recoverability quantifies structure captured by the specified linear model and does not establish independent molecular information. Source data are provided with the figure.
