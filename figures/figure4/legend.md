**Fig. 4 | Pathway classification using transcriptomic and inferred regulatory features.** **a,** Overview of the pathway-classification workflow. Gene-expression profiles from the perturbational samples were used alone or concatenated with inferred transcription-factor activity (TFA), inferred miRNA expression (HubmiR), or both, and classified into 11 pathway classes using random forest (RF), multilayer perceptron (MLP), Kolmogorov–Arnold network (KAN) and residual network (ResNet) models. **b,c,** Held-out test accuracy (**b**) and macro-F1 (**c**) for Gene (green), Gene + HubmiR (blue), Gene + TFA (orange) and all three feature layers (grey). Each dot represents one held-out test result from a stratified split. Boxes show the median and interquartile range, whiskers span the minimum and maximum, and black diamonds with error bars denote the mean ± s.d. (n = 7 displayed random-seed splits per model and feature space). Dashed horizontal lines show the mean performance of the gene-only PROGENy linear pathway-activity model over 7 splits.
