**Fig. 5 | Representative pathway-classification rescues associated with inferred HubmiR features.** The upper schematic provides biological context for the EGFR/MAPK and hypoxia–HIF programmes and their represented perturbations; it is conceptual and does not assert direct regulation by inferred miRNAs. The lower heatmaps show nine strict rescue profiles—three each for EGFR, MAPK and hypoxia—in which the Gene-only ResNet prediction was incorrect and Gene + HubmiR was correct on the same held-out split. Upward and downward arrows denote activating and inhibiting perturbations, respectively. Within each pathway, the profiles share eight Gene and three inferred-HubmiR columns. Genes were selected from the top 60 official PROGENy footprints to minimize the largest absolute fold-specific deviation across the three profiles (absolute deviation ≤ 0.5). Predefined pathway-linked miRNAs were retained and additional HubmiRs completed each three-feature panel. Cells show the mean training-fold empirical-percentile deviation across strict-rescue occurrences, calculated as 2 × (empirical percentile − 0.5); values range from −1 to +1, zero denotes the training-fold median, and blue-to-red indicates relatively low-to-high values. The red frame marks the Gene-only view and the green frame Gene + HubmiR. Profiles and non-anchor HubmiRs were selected descriptively after evaluation from random-seed held-out predictions; the heatmaps are exploratory cases rather than an unbiased population estimate. No hypothesis test is shown. HubmiR values are model-inferred proxies, and these cases do not establish causal miRNA-mediated rescue or independent molecular information. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
