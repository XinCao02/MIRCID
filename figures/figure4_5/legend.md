**Fig. 4.5 | Generic embedding controls and complementary geometry of the inferred HubmiR representation.** **a,** Held-out macro-F1 for RF and ResNet using Gene, Gene plus 414-dimensional principal-component (PCA), autoencoder (AE) or Gaussian random-projection (RP) controls, HubmiR alone, or Gene + HubmiR. Controls used the same 977 landmark-gene inputs as HubmiR; RP averages five projections. Boxes show the median and interquartile range (n = 7), whiskers the minimum and maximum, circles individual runs, and diamonds with bars the mean ± s.d. **b,** Regularized canonical correlation analysis between Gene and each representation after outer-training standardization and reduction to 64 principal components (ridge = 0.1). Lines and bands show the mean and bootstrap 95% confidence interval across 20 frozen splits for the first ten modes. **c,** Debiased linear and radial-basis-function (RBF) centred-kernel alignment (CKA) with Gene; RBF bandwidths were derived from training data. **d,** Held-out variance-weighted R² from multi-output ridge regression predicting HubmiR from full Gene or the 977-gene input; regularization was selected by fivefold inner cross-validation. **e,** Paired change in held-out macro-F1 for RF, MLP, KAN and ResNet after adding HubmiR to Gene, or Gene to HubmiR alone. Circles show split-wise differences, diamonds means and lines minimum–maximum ranges; zero denotes no change. Panels **a,e** show n = 7 held-out random-seed runs per group or contrast. CKA, CCA and ridge predictability measure shared or recoverable structure, not independent molecular information.
